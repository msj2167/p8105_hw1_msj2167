## Problem 1:

### Data set description

To do this problem, I must load the tidyverse library and the penguins
dataset

``` r
data("penguins", package = "palmerpenguins")
```

The penguins data set has 344 rows and 8 columns. Each row is one
penguin and the variables are species, island, bill_length_mm,
bill_depth_mm, flipper_length_mm, body_mass_g, sex, year.

Penguins were observed in one of three islands Biscoe, Dream, Torgersen
and belong to one of three species Adelie, Chinstrap, Gentoo. They were
seen between 2007 and 2009. The physical measurements of penguins were
recorded and included bill length and depth (mm), flipper length (mm),
and body mass(g). The sex of the penguins were also recorded.

The mean flipper length is 200.92
